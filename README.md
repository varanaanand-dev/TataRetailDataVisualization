# Tata Retail Data Visualization 

### Retail Analytics | Tableau | Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights** virtual experience on Forage.

The objective was to explore retail sales data and communicate business insights through Tableau visualizations designed to support decision-making.

## Business Questions

The visualization exercise focused on four business perspectives:

1. **Revenue Trends:** Understanding how revenue changes over time.
2. **Geographic Performance:** Comparing revenue across countries.
3. **Customer Performance:** Identifying high-value customers.
4. **Product Demand:** Exploring demand patterns across countries.

## Tools & Skills

- Tableau — data visualization and dashboard creation
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Retail sales analysis
- Business intelligence and data storytelling
